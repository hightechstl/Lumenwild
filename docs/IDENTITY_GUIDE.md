# Lumenwild identity guide

## Voice and audience

Warm, curious, capable, and age-neutral. Use direct language that respects younger readers without talking down to them and feels natural to adults. Never assign gender to a player or Glimmer. Avoid gender-coded collections, “for boys/girls” presentation, baby-talk, romance, or beauty-as-progress framing.

## Visual system

- Concept: adventurous field journal with paper-cut depth and hand-inked botanical detail.
- Colors: Parchment `#FFF9EB`, ink plum `#35262D`, moss `#45634C`, moon teal `#418C83`, ember coral `#DD755F`, trail gold `#D8A743`.
- Type: Fraunces for welcoming display type; DM Sans for readable interface text. System fallbacks preserve usability offline.
- Geometry: open layouts, purposeful paper panels, 12–24px radii, thin warm borders, restrained earthen shadows.
- Motifs: four-point star, leaf study, map contour, stitched edge, field-note label.

## Original terminology

Game: Lumenwild. Creatures: Glimmers. World: Bramblewake Archipelago. Region: Mossmere. Earned currency: Dewdrops. Cosmetic currency: Starpetals. Daily activity: Daily Glow. Personal room: Nook. Social groups (future): Circles. Minigame: Skyhop. Shops: The Forage Fold and Nook & Notion.

## Originality review

All titles, species, NPCs, geography, economy names, objects, dialogue, and visual motifs were created for this project. The design avoids pixel-device nostalgia, capsule-shaped handheld framing, battle-centric monster presentation, nursery styling, and recognizable species silhouettes or franchise UI structures. “Virtual pet,” needs meters, inventories, shops, rooms, exploration, and minigames are genre-level mechanics; their names, combination, art, layouts, and fiction here are original. The Spriggle’s leaf ears and plume tail, the Mallowisp’s mist anatomy and fin ears, and the Bramblet’s six-legged bark plates were reviewed as a cohesive original trio.
